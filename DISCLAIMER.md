# Disclaimer: Risks of Automating Facebook

**Read this before you use TaskPilot (The Engager 2).** If you use the app, you accept these risks.

## 1. Facebook's rules
- Facebook's [Terms of Service](https://www.facebook.com/terms.php) and [Community Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/) limit automated access. They also forbid "inauthentic behavior", which includes coordinated or artificial likes, comments and shares.
- Even with human approval, Facebook may treat browser automation on your account as against its terms.
- **This app is not made, approved or supported by Meta or Facebook.**

## 2. What can happen to your account
Facebook detects automated activity. The more actions you take and the faster you take them, the more likely it notices. It may:
- ask you to prove you are a person (a security check or CAPTCHA), or ask you to log in again
- **block** liking, commenting or sharing for a while ("You're temporarily blocked")
- hide your comments or shares, or limit who can see them
- lock, suspend or **permanently disable** your account. **You could lose your account and everything in it.**

Facebook can change how it detects automation at any time and without notice. A setup that worked yesterday can be flagged today.

## 3. What the app does and does not do
**The app does:**
- ask for your approval before every run
- act only on posts that you list yourself
- check before acting that **your own** account is signed in
- refuse to repeat an action on the same post
- count an action as done only when Facebook shows it, and keep screenshots as proof

**The app does not:**
- bypass CAPTCHAs, security checks, two-factor authentication or any other platform security; it **stops** when it meets one
- run on fake accounts, extra accounts or shared accounts
- store your Facebook password; you sign in yourself in the app's browser

**These safeguards lower the risk but do not remove it.** Using automation on your account is your choice and your responsibility.

## 4. How to use it responsibly
- Use only **your own, real** account. Never use someone else's account or a fake one.
- Engage only with posts you actually support. Use comments that are honest and that you would write yourself.
- Keep each run small, and space your runs out. Do not run it around the clock or on many PCs with the same account.
- If Facebook shows a warning, a block or a security check, **stop**. Wait at least 24 to 48 hours, then continue by hand.
- Follow your organization's rules on social media use and official accounts.
- Check the results yourself before you submit any Google Form.

## 5. Facebook changes its pages
Facebook changes its pages often. After a change, the app may stop finding buttons, or it may report an action as "unverified" or "failed". In that case:
- check the post by hand
- never report an action as done unless you can see it on the post

## 6. Your data
- Your login session, your details and the screenshots stay on **your PC**. Your details are stored encrypted.
- Anyone who can use your PC may be able to use your signed-in session. Lock your PC.
- Delete the upload folders when you no longer need them. The dashboard has an option for this.
